DIGITAL FLORA

Advanced plant models for a changing world.

We develop AI-powered Advanced Plant Models—high-fidelity digital twins that capture the physiological complexity of diverse agricultural species.

By leveraging artificial intelligence to integrate real-time environmental data, computer vision, and biological modeling, our systems accurately predict yield, growth dynamics, and plant health.

These models enable yield forecasting and treatment optimization, maximizing productivity while reducing operational costs and environmental impact.
